Part 01

How are you?

This is how you ask someone how they are. Men and women use different polite endings, so read the card that applies to you.

If you are a man
สบายดีไหมครับ
sà-baai dee măi kráp
How are you?
If you are a woman
สบายดีไหมคะ
sà-baai dee măi ká
How are you?
A common reply

Whoever asks, the usual answer is the same short phrase — and it works for men and women alike:

สบายดี
sà-baai dee
I'm fine.

Add your polite particle to the end when you speak to a stranger or someone older than you: สบายดีครับ sà-baai dee kráp for men, สบายดีค่ะ sà-baai dee kâ for women.

Part 02

And you?

After answering a question, you can hand it straight back with one short phrase.

แล้วคุณล่ะ
láew kun lâ
And you?
Polite particles

Remember to add the appropriate polite particle when speaking to strangers or people older than you.

ครับ
kráp
For male speakers — the same in questions and statements.
คะ
ká
For female speakers — high tone, for questions.
ค่ะ
kâ
For female speakers — falling tone, for statements.

So a woman asking แล้วคุณล่ะคะ láew kun lâ ká ends on the high tone, but answering สบายดีค่ะ sà-baai dee kâ she ends on the falling tone. Men use ครับ kráp for both.

Listen carefully. Mali asks Achara how she is, and Achara asks her back.

Mali
สบายดีไหมคะ
sà-baai dee măi ká
How are you?
Achara
สบายดีค่ะ
sà-baai dee kâ
I'm fine.
ขอบคุณค่ะ
kòp kun kâ
Thank you.
แล้วคุณล่ะคะ
láew kun lâ ká
And you?
Mali
สบายดีค่ะ
sà-baai dee kâ
I'm fine.
ขอบคุณค่ะ
kòp kun kâ
Thank you.
Part 03

Where are you from?

Tap play to hear each question, then repeat it out loud.

If you are a man
คุณมาจากที่ไหนครับ
kun maa jàak têe năi kráp
Where are you from?
If you are a woman
คุณมาจากที่ไหนคะ
kun maa jàak têe năi ká
Where are you from?
To answer, if you are a man
ผมมาจาก … ครับ
pŏm maa jàak your country kráp
I'm from …
To answer, if you are a woman
ฉันมาจาก … ค่ะ
chăn maa jàak your country kâ
I'm from …

Listen carefully to the conversation between Mali and David. Try to catch the question and the country in David's answer — then listen once more and say his line along with him.

Mali
ชื่ออะไรคะ
chûu à-rai ká
What's your name?
David
ชื่อเดวิดครับ
chûu David kráp
My name is David.
Mali
เดวิดมาจากไหนคะ
David maa jàak năi ká
Where are you from, David?
David
มาจากอังกฤษครับ
maa jàak ang-grìt kráp
I'm from England.

Notice that the personal pronouns are left out here — leaving out คุณ kun and ผม pŏm is very common in everyday spoken Thai whenever it is already clear who is being talked about.

Part 04

Introduce another person

You also learned how to introduce someone else — your partner, a friend, or a family member.

ฉันชื่อโซเฟียค่ะ
chăn chûu Sophia kâ
My name is Sophia.
เขาชื่อเปโดร
kăo chûu Pedro
His name is Pedro.
เรามาจากสเปนค่ะ
rao maa jàak Spain kâ
We are from Spain.

เขา kăo covers both he and she, so the same sentence works whoever you are introducing.

เขาชื่อจูเลีย
kăo chûu Julia
Her name is Julia.

Part 07

Homework

Besides learning the words taught in this lesson, here's a short listening exercise for your homework.
